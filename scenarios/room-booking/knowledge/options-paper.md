# Options paper: replacing the room booking spreadsheet

_Simulated document for the platform demo._

## Option A: Outlook room mailboxes

Each room becomes a room mailbox in the university's existing Microsoft 365 tenant, with automatic accept or decline so overlapping bookings are refused. Staff book from Outlook by adding the room to a meeting. Room capacity and wheelchair access can be set as room properties; AV equipment can be recorded in the room's description but cannot be filtered on. Microsoft 365 is already licensed for all staff, so there is no extra licence cost. The IS collaboration team estimates 15 days of set-up at A$1,100 a day, then about 0.05 FTE a year to maintain rooms. Microsoft 365 data for the university is stored in Australia. Out of the box there is no report on bookings not used.

## Option B: BookIt (software as a service)

BookIt is a hosted room booking product. It has a floor plan view, filters for capacity, AV equipment and access, check-in at the room so unused bookings are released, and a monthly usage report. It syncs bookings to Outlook calendars and supports single sign-on with Microsoft Entra ID. BookIt costs A$6 per room per month, with a one-off implementation fee of A$8,000. Ongoing administration is estimated at 0.1 FTE a year.

## Assessment

BookIt meets all of the Faculty's requirements and is the recommended option.

## Vendor notes (from the BookIt sales call)

BookIt hosts all customer data in its United States region. An Australian region is planned but has no date. Implementation typically takes eight to ten weeks from contract signature.
