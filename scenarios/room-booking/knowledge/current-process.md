# Current room booking process

_Simulated document for the platform demo._

## How it works today

The Faculty books its 40 meeting rooms in one shared spreadsheet on the Faculty's SharePoint site. Staff add a row with the room, date, start and end time, their name and the purpose. Two administrators check the sheet each morning and email staff when two bookings overlap.

## Problems

The spreadsheet does not stop two people booking the same room at the same time. The administrators logged an average of 12 double bookings a month over the last six months.

Bookings cannot be seen from Outlook, so staff check the spreadsheet and their calendar separately. Rooms are often booked and not used; nobody records whether a booking was used.

The administrators spend about 0.3 FTE in total on room bookings, mostly resolving clashes.

## Volumes

There are about 650 bookings a month across the 40 rooms. About 220 staff book rooms at least once a year.
