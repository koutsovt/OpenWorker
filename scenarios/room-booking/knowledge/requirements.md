# Requirements for room booking

_Simulated document for the platform demo._

RB-01. The system must prevent two bookings of the same room for overlapping times.

RB-02. Bookings must appear in the booker's Outlook calendar without a separate step.

RB-03. Staff must be able to search rooms by capacity, AV equipment and wheelchair access.

RB-04. Staff must sign in with their university account (single sign-on). No separate passwords.

RB-05. Booking records include staff names and email addresses, which are personal information. They must be stored in Australia, in line with the university's data residency standard.

RB-06. Administrators must be able to report monthly on room use and on bookings not used.

RB-07. The solution should be live before the start of next semester, in about four months.
