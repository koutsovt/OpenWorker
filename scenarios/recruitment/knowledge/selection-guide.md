# Selection guide

_Simulated document for the platform demo._

## Rating scale

Rate every applicant against every selection criterion, using only the evidence in their application.

3, strong: specific examples with what the applicant did and the result, closely matching the criterion.

2, meets: a clear example or a relevant role that shows the criterion is met.

1, limited: a general claim with little or no specific example, or experience that only partly matches.

0, no evidence: the application does not address the criterion.

## How to count experience

For E2, count the years in roles where business analysis was the main duty. Roles where it was a minor part, such as data analysis, reporting or teaching, do not count towards the five years, but may be relevant to other criteria.

A career break does not reduce the experience gained before or after it. Count the years actually worked in business analysis.

## How to apply the ratings

To be shortlisted, an applicant must be rated 2 or more on every essential criterion (E1 to E6).

Among applicants who meet every essential criterion, rank by the total of the essential ratings. Where totals are equal or within one point, the desirable criteria (D1, D2) decide.

The reserve is the next-ranked applicant who meets every essential criterion.

A claim without an example rates at most 1. Titles, adjectives and the size of an organisation are not evidence of skill.

For E6, rate the clarity and structure of the application as a whole. Occasional spelling or typing errors lower the rating only if they make the meaning unclear.
