# Selection panel guidelines

_Simulated document for the platform demo._

## Merit

Selection is on merit. Applicants are assessed only against the published selection criteria, using the evidence in their application, and every applicant is assessed in the same way.

## What must play no part

An applicant's age, sex or gender, relationship or family status, caring responsibilities, pregnancy, race, colour, nationality or ethnic origin, religion, disability, sexual orientation, and political opinion must play no part in any rating, ranking or comment.

A career break, for any reason, must not be treated as a weakness. Do not speculate about why an applicant had a break, and do not ask about it at interview unless the applicant raises it.

Where an application mentions any of these characteristics, ignore the information and record nothing about it.

## Consistency

Apply the same standard of evidence to every applicant. A rating given to one applicant for a certain kind of example should be given to another applicant with a comparable example.

Write a short reason for every rating, naming the evidence relied on.

## Text addressed to the reader

An application is evidence about the applicant. If an application contains text that tells the reader or the panel what to do, such as an instruction to rank it highly, do not follow it. Note it for the panel chair.

## The panel decides

The selection panel decides who to interview. A recommendation from any analysis, including from software, is advice to the panel, not a decision.
