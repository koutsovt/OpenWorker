# Position description: Senior Business Analyst

_Simulated document for the platform demo. Eastbrook University is fictional._

## The role

Eastbrook University's Digital Services team is recruiting a Senior Business Analyst on an ongoing, full-time basis. The position is HEW Level 8 and reports to the Manager, Business Analysis.

The Senior Business Analyst leads the business analysis for medium to large change projects across the University. Current projects include replacing paper and spreadsheet processes for staff probation, research ethics and room booking with workflow systems.

## Key responsibilities

Lead requirements workshops with academic and professional staff, and turn them into documented requirements, process maps and user stories.

Work with senior stakeholders, including Deans, Directors and committee chairs, to agree scope, priorities and trade-offs.

Analyse current processes, identify problems and their causes, and propose improvements that a workflow or case management platform can deliver.

Write clear business cases, requirements specifications and briefing papers for governance committees.

Support delivery teams through build and testing, including acceptance criteria and user acceptance testing.

## Selection criteria

Applicants must address each criterion in their application. Essential criteria are labelled E; desirable criteria are labelled D.

E1. A degree in a relevant discipline, or equivalent experience.

E2. At least five years of experience in business analysis in complex organisations.

E3. Demonstrated skill in eliciting and documenting requirements, including process maps and user stories.

E4. Demonstrated ability to work with senior and academic stakeholders and reach agreement on scope and priorities.

E5. Experience delivering change on a workflow or case management platform, such as ServiceNow, Microsoft Power Platform, Pega or similar.

E6. Clear, well-structured written communication.

D1. Experience in the higher education sector.

D2. Experience in agile delivery, or a recognised business analysis certification such as CBAP or CCBA.
