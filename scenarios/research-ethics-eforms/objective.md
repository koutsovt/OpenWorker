Research Ethics eForms Modernisation.

Assess replacing the university's Research Ethics eForms, which run on Power Apps and Dataverse, with a
Laravel and PostgreSQL solution on open-source hosting: run locally first, then on Railway. Salesforce
PRIME is retained and must keep receiving approved applications.

Produce: the target architecture and the decisions behind it; a security threat model with controls;
the data model, the data migration approach and the PRIME integration approach; and an indicative
cost view with stated assumptions. Base every claim about the current system, the requirements and
the costs on the knowledge documents, and say where they are silent.
